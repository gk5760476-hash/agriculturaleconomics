# Policy framework

What the evidence supports, what it does not, and what to investigate next. Read with [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md).

## What the data indicate
1. **No countercyclical rise in the season after a dry monsoon** in Bihar or Maharashtra districts (2010–2026). The interval excludes the 3–5% rise found nationally for 1993–2010.
2. **In Bihar, agricultural credit is lower 2–4 years after a district's monsoon shortfall**, through **fewer borrowers**, mainly at **regional rural banks** and **rural branches**. This is an association; the mechanism (reduced lending, lower demand, repayment problems or restructuring) is not identified.
3. **Maharashtra shows no clear response.** State-level series are too short to decide.

## Policy questions this raises (hypotheses, not conclusions)
| Pattern | Question to investigate | Who could check |
|---|---|---|
| Bihar borrower numbers fall 2–3 years after drought | Do drought-hit borrowers become overdue/NPA and lose access to fresh KCC limits? | NABARD, Bihar SLBC, RRB sponsor banks |
| Concentrated in RRBs | Are RRB balance sheets or capital limits binding after local droughts? | NABARD (RRB supervision) |
| No immediate rise anywhere | Are RBI natural-calamity relief measures (rescheduling, fresh loans) being invoked and reaching borrowers? | RBI, district lead banks |
| Weak link between shocks and credit overall | Is credit supply driven by targets (priority-sector, KCC drives) rather than local conditions? | State planning departments |

## Decision table (spec s.13.2), applied
| Observed | Interpretation to investigate | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| District on **Watch** (deficient monsoon) | Credit may not keep pace with distress | Check disbursement timing and new KCC sanctions in that district |
| Bihar district on **Watch (lagged credit risk)** | Borrower exits may follow 2–3 years later | Monitor overdue accounts and renewals with the lead bank |
| **Data insufficient** | The signal is unreliable | Wait for yield and credit data; do not act on a partial signal |

## Safeguards
The system must not be used to deny credit, rank communities as risky, infer individual repayment behaviour, allege bank misconduct, or justify waivers or compulsory lending without separate evaluation (spec s.13.3). Every flag means "verify locally", not "act".
